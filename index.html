About

Hi, I'm Joy. I teach machines to see traffic the way it actually moves on Philippine streets.

At DOST-ASTI I build and fine-tune detection models for traffic monitoring, lead data engineering on datasets from our stakeholders, and supervise interns. I also teach undergraduate Python as part-time faculty at Mapúa University.

My graduate research, as a DOST-ERDT scholar, is a vision-based traffic signal that spots emergency response vehicles at T-junctions and gives them the green. Outside the lab I lead IEEE CS Philippines, bringing topics like quantum computing and smart cities to students around the country.
